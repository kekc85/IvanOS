# Viral Hooks 2026 (X) - 15 patterns

> Base 8 + 7 new from 21-author research (2026-05-28).

## Base library (8 patterns)

### 1. Counter-intuitive opener
**Template:** "Most [niche] advice is wrong."
**Authors:** Levels / Hormozi / Sanchez
**Examples:**
- "Most indie hacking advice is wrong. Build for $1000/mo first, not $100K." - Levels
- "Most 'sales objections' are excuses you accept." - Hormozi

### 2. Confession + pivot
**Template:** "I spent [time] on [X] and earned $0. Then I changed [3 things]."
**Authors:** Welsh / Marc Lou / Friks / Jack Friks
**Examples:**
- "I lied about [X] for [Y months]. Here's what worked." - Lara Acosta
- "I spent 4 years writing on LinkedIn. Then I changed one thing." - Welsh

### 3. Specific outcome + mystery
**Template:** "I went from $0 to $[N]/mo. Here's the workflow."
**Authors:** Marc Lou / Levels / Friks
**Examples:**
- "I made $103,000 in May. Here's the breakdown:" - Marc Lou
- "I just passed $50K MRR on Photo AI. Workflow:" - Levels

### 4. Numbered promise
**Template:** "N specific [topic] mistakes (and the fix)"
**Authors:** Welsh / Cole / Bloom
**Examples:**
- "5 X tweets you should write every week." - Welsh
- "10 mental models that will save you years of bad decisions." - Bloom

### 5. PAIPS opening
**Template:** "I was [pain reality]. Then [pivot]."
**Author:** Welsh signature
**Example:**
- "I was fired with 2 kids and $0 in savings. There was no playbook for what came next." - Welsh

### 6. Greentext mystery
**Template:** "anon discovers [absurd insight]. screenshots inside."
**Author:** Cheung (AI avatar reveal pattern)
**Example:**
- "AI NEWS: Microsoft just added Copilot to every Office app. Here's why it matters." - Cheung adaptation

### 7. "I asked N customers"
**Template:** "I asked N customers why they paid $2K+"
**Authors:** Acosta / Isenberg / Sanchez
**Example:**
- "I asked 100 of my customers why they paid $2K+. Here's what 87 of them said." - Acosta

### 8. Authority dunk
**Template:** Quote-tweet wrong claim + 1-line correction
**Authors:** Levels / Hormozi
**Example:**
- [Quote-tweet "AI will replace all jobs"] "Actually it will replace specific tasks. Knowing which tasks = the play."

## Extended library (7 new, MECE-collapsed from B6 30 flagged + cross-bucket)

### 9. Day-counter X/Y framing
**Template:** "Day [X]/[Y]: shipped [outcome]"
**Author:** Friks (build-in-public signature)
**Example:**
- "Day 47/90 of MakerHQ. $11K → $103K MRR. 3 things that worked:" - Friks
**автор RU:** "День 47/90 запуска курса. Регистраций: 1396. Что собрал за неделю:"

### 10. Parallel anaphora "it's like X, but Y"
**Template:** "[A] is like [B], but [twist]. [A2] is like [B2], but [twist]..." × 5 repetitions
**Author:** Yacine
**Example:**
- "Cursor is like nvim but slow. VSCode is like nvim but bloated. AI Editor is like nvim but distracted..."

### 11. Frustration-as-flex
**Template:** "I don't understand why [common practice]. [Counter-fact]."
**Authors:** Yacine / Ras Mic
**Example:**
- "I don't understand why people pay $500K for a course when Claude Code is free."

### 12. Meta-authority frame
**Template:** "Most [X] gloss over [Y]. This one doesn't."
**Author:** Brett Malinowski
**Example:**
- "Most courses on AI gloss over the hard part: shipping. Mine doesn't."

### 13. Named-Principle Visual Thread
**Template:** "Everyone needs to read this. The [Name] Principle. (a visual thread)"
**Author:** Bloom (weekly reuse template)
**Example:**
- "Everyone needs to read this. The Parent Time Principle. (a visual thread)" - Bloom

### 14. Naked aphorism
**Template:** 3-15 word identity bomb, no setup, no proof
**Authors:** Naval / Iman / Butcher
**Examples:**
- "Earn with your mind, not your time." - Naval
- "If 18-year-old me saw 28-year-old me, he'd be proud." - Iman
- "Complexity happens by default. Simplicity happens by design." - Butcher

### 15. "$X/mo idea for you"
**Template:** "$[N]/mo idea for you: [1-2 line concept]"
**Author:** Isenberg (signature daily format)
**Example:**
- "$10K/mo idea for you: AI-driven podcast clip generator for solopreneurs."

## Score per variant (use в 02-structure.md Step 2)

Generate 3 hook variants → score each:
- **Specificity** (concrete number / identity): 0-3
- **Novelty** (counter-intuitive / fresh): 0-3
- **Voice match** (автора primary register): 0-2
- **Hook readiness** (первая фраза готова): 0-2

Total /10. Pick top 1 для primary, top 2-3 для A/B variants.
