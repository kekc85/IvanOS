# Template: PAIPS story

**Reference cluster:** PAIPS-story (Welsh signature, validated в research per-author/en-welsh-x.md).
**Char zone:** Hook = B (200-280) preferred.
**Format:** Mini-thread 5-8 tweets OR X Premium long 150-300 слов.

## Skeleton (mini-thread)

```
1/ [Pain hook - concrete pain reality, B 200-280 chars]
2/ [Agitate - consequences, what happens when not solved]
3/ [Intrigue - counter-intuitive turn: "But the worst day was..."]
4/ [Positive - solution / lesson learned]
5/ [Closer - soft CTA: newsletter / community / follow / null]
```

## Hook patterns applicable

5. PAIPS opening (Welsh signature)
2. Confession + pivot

## Voice rules

- Personal pain (real, not invented)
- 1-2 строка emotional hook
- 2-3 line breaks between paragraphs (mobile-readable)
- "I" first-person
- Concrete numbers / dates / people
- Vulnerable but не whining

## Verbatim Welsh example

```
1/ I was fired with 2 kids and $0 in savings.

2/ There was no playbook for what came next.
   Every day felt like I was failing in a new way.

3/ But the worst day became my best decision.
   I stopped looking for a job - I started building one.

4/ Here's what I learned about the 1-person business in 8 years:

[5-7 numbered insights]

8/ If you want the full playbook, my newsletter goes deep.
```

## автор RU adaptation example

```
1/ В 2024 году я слил 2 миллиона на курсы по продюсированию.

2/ Каждый запуск шёл по той же схеме: дорого, медленно, без результата.

3/ А потом я понял что проблема не в скорости запусков.
   Проблема в том что я строю чужой бизнес - а не свой.

4/ Вот что изменилось когда я переключился на вайбмаркетинг:

[5-7 numbered shifts]

8/ Если хочешь полный разбор - подписывайся на канал.
```

## Anti-patterns

- Invented pain (только real)
- Hyperbolic ("changed my life forever")
- Hard sell CTA в финале
- Without verbatim numbers / dates
- Pure motivational ("just believe in yourself")
- LinkedIn emoji-спам (LinkedIn ≠ X)
